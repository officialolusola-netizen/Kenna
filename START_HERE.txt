🚀 QUICK START (3 STEPS):

  1. Double-click "index.html" or "nigerian_tax_calculator.html"

  2. It will open in your default web browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox, etc.)

  3. Enter salary information and click "Calculate PAYE"

================================================================================

💡 TROUBLESHOOTING:

  Problem: I see code/text instead of the calculator
  Solution: You opened the wrong file! Open index.html instead

  Problem: Nothing happens when I click
  Solution: Make sure JavaScript is enabled in your browser

  Problem: File won't open
  Solution: Right-click → Open with → Choose your web browser

📊 WHAT GETS CALCULATED:

  ✅ PAYE (Personal Income Tax)
  ✅ Tax-free status (if income ≤₦800,000)
  ✅ Rent Relief (20% of rent, max ₦500k)
  ✅ Monthly and annual net salary
  ✅ Detailed tax breakdown per band

================================================================================

🇳🇬 Based on Nigeria Tax Act, 2025 (Effective January 1, 2026)

Tax Bands:
  • First ₦800,000 → 0% (TAX-FREE!)
  • ₦800k - ₦3M → 15%
  • ₦3M - ₦10M → 18%
  • ₦10M - ₦25M → 21%
  • ₦25M - ₦50M → 23%
  • Above ₦50M → 25%
