📋 Nigeria Tax Act 2025 - PAYE Tax Bands

The new progressive tax rates with the first ₦800,000 completely tax-free!

Taxable Income Range Tax Rate
First ₦800,000 0% (Tax-Free! 🎉)
₦800,001 - ₦3,000,000 15%
₦3,000,001 - ₦10,000,000 18%
₦10,000,001 - ₦25,000,000 21%
₦25,000,001 - ₦50,000,000 23%
Above ₦50,000,000 25%
Rent Relief: You can claim 20% of annual rent paid, capped at ₦500,000 (replaces the old Consolidated Relief Allowance)
